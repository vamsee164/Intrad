Intra-D is an agricultural platform bridging the gap between farmers, sellers, and buyers — leveraging technology to bring prosperity to rural India.

Our Mission

To digitally connect farmers with markets, tools, and resources — removing middlemen, reducing costs, and maximizing farm income through transparent trade and smart agriculture services.

Our Vision

A future where every Indian farmer has equal access to technology, fair prices, modern equipment, and expert guidance — making agriculture a thriving and dignified profession.
